# Example Service Handbook

This fictional handbook is included only to demonstrate document ingestion.

## Availability

The example service targets 99.5% monthly availability. Planned maintenance is
announced at least 24 hours in advance.

## Support

Support is available on weekdays from 09:00 to 17:00 UTC. Urgent incidents should
be reported through the service status page.

## Data handling

Do not upload passwords, access tokens, or personal data to a demonstration
environment. Use synthetic data when evaluating the gateway.
