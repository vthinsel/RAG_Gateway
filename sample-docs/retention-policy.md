# Example Records Retention Policy

This fictional policy is a demonstration corpus, not legal or operational advice.

## Retention periods

- Support requests are retained for 30 days after closure.
- Service audit events are retained for 90 days.
- A user may request deletion of their profile through the account settings page.

## Backups

Encrypted backups are retained for 14 days. Deleting a record from the active
service does not immediately remove copies already present in a backup.

## Questions

For this example, the answer to "How long are audit events kept?" is 90 days.
